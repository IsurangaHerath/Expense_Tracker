## Document Conventions

- **Requirement IDs**: Format FR-MOD-### for functional requirements
- **Traceability Matrix**: Links requirements to use cases and acceptance criteria
- **Use Cases**: Include complete flow descriptions with alternative/error paths
- **Measurements**: All requirements include quantifiable metrics where possible
